## Week 3 (Mar 23–29)

### Post 7 — Seasonal Tip (Monday Mar 23)
**Type:** What's New

**Text:**
Spring cleaning tip: don't forget your garage door tracks!

Dirt, grease buildup, and debris in the tracks can cause your door to bind or come off track. Here's what to do:

1. Wipe down the tracks with a damp cloth (no lubricant on the tracks!)
2. Check for dents or bends — even minor ones cause problems
3. Lubricate the rollers, hinges, and springs (NOT the tracks)

Use a silicone or lithium-based lubricant — not WD-40, which can actually attract more dirt.

Need help? Our $49 spring tune-up handles all of this. Book before March 31st!
